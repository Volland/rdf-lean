# Privacy

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds nothing, and collects nothing about you. The long version follows, because GDPR asks for the long version.

## What runs on this site

No analytics. No tracking pixels. No cookies — not functional ones, not any others. No embedded videos, newsletter widgets, comment systems, or social buttons. No consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

The site loads no web fonts: it uses your device's system fonts, and its stylesheet and images are served from this site's own server, so loading a page does not disclose your IP address to a font CDN or any other third party.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Serving a page necessarily involves GitHub processing your IP address and request metadata in their server logs — this is technically unavoidable for any website and is the legal basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in operating the site. I have no access to those logs. GitHub's privacy statement: [docs.github.com](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Links away from this site

Reading the source code, downloading a release or trying oxilite all happen somewhere else — GitHub, oxilitedb.com and the W3C, among others. Once you follow such a link, that provider's privacy policy applies and this one stops. Nothing is loaded from those services while you are on this site; they are ordinary links.

I keep correspondence as long as it is useful for the enquiry or as long as commercial retention obligations require, and I do not add you to any mailing list.
