LeanRDF 0.3.0 adds statement graphs: tiramemsu's bitemporal statement records, an exact export to RDF 1.2 reification, and the results of *When Is a Temporal Statement Store a Statement Graph?* (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23335652), proven over LeanRDF's own terms. The new package is `leanrdf-statements` in `statements/`. It requires only the core, and nothing else changed.

## What is new

- **Records:**
  - `Record` holds an eid, a subject and an object that are each a term or another record's eid, a predicate IRI, a transaction interval and a valid-time interval in epoch milliseconds.
  - A `StatementGraph` has distinct eids. Dangling references and cycles are allowed; self-reference is not.
- **Well-formedness:** `WellFormed` is decidable and `check` lists located violations:
  - strict RDF 1.2 positions;
  - sane intervals;
  - no self-reference;
  - the export's reserved names (`urn:tiramemsu:stmt:N`, `rdf:reifies`, the four `tm:` time predicates) kept out of content.
- **Views:** `now`, `asOf t` or `history`, with an optional valid-time point. Whether a record is asserted follows from its clocks and the view.
- **Export:** `toRDF v` writes strict RDF 1.2. Each record becomes a reifier `stmt:e rdf:reifies <<( s p o )>>` with its clocks as `xsd:integer` literals, plus its base triple when it is live in the view.
- **Exact decoding:**
  - `decode_toRDF : decode v (sg.toRDF v) = .ok sg`;
  - `toRDF_decode`: a graph that decodes is exactly the export of a well-formed statement graph.
- **The paper, vendored and instantiated:**
  - `criterion`: a direct export to a Gelling–Fletcher–Schmidt Statement Graph exists exactly when the records are closed and acyclic, and it is unique.
  - `kernel`, an executable peel proven equal to the paper's export kernel (`mem_kernel_iff`, `kernel_greatest`), with the deciders `isClosed` and `isAcyclic`.
  - History closure (`history_closed`, `history_cycle`, `history_kernel`).
  - The quoting view into generalized RDF 1.2 (`quote`, `quotes_injective'`, `bgp_view'`).
- **Command line:** `leanrdf-sg check | export | decode | kernel | quote` over JSON-lines records.
- **Tests:**
  - every spec scenario;
  - golden exports per view, marked "pending tm cross-check" until tiramemsu writes the format;
  - an exhaustive check over all 406,901 statement graphs of at most four records.
- **Example:** `examples/statement-graphs/`, a corrected flight distance across views, decoding, and a tampered export that is rejected.

## Breaking changes

None. Every package moves to version 0.3.0, and CI gains a job for `statements/`.

## What is proven and what is trusted

- **Proven:** every theorem is checked by Lean's kernel. No `sorry`, and only `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`.
- **Trusted, not proven:**
  - The transcription of the Statement Graph definition. It is the paper's, vendored from tiramemsu at commit `55735c3`, with only the imports changed.
  - The JSON-lines reader and the N-Triples parser, which are tested.
  - The Lean compiler, for the command line and the exhaustive check.
- **Not mechanized:** the linear-time bound of the kernel algorithm.
- **Not yet cross-checked:** tiramemsu's own `tm export` does not write this format yet.
