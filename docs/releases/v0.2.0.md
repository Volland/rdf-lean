LeanRDF 0.2.0 moves the whole library to RDF 1.2: triples can be terms, statements can be reified and annotated, and language-tagged strings can carry a base direction. The model theory follows RDF 1.2 Semantics, and every soundness theorem kept its statement.

## What is new

- **Data model:**
  - `Term.triple s p o` for triple terms and `Literal.dirLang` for directional language-tagged strings (`ltr`/`rtl`).
  - Strict RDF 1.2 well-formedness: a triple term may only be an object.
  - `Basic` predicates for RDF 1.2 Basic conformance.
  - Blank-node renaming and isomorphism reach inside triple terms.
- **Syntax:**
  - N-Triples and N-Quads 1.2, with canonical RDF 1.2 output that escapes control characters.
  - Turtle and TriG 1.2, with `<< s p o ~ r >>` reified triples, `~ r {| … |}` annotations and `VERSION` directives, all expanded into `rdf:reifies` triples while parsing.
  - The W3C RDF 1.2 N-Triples, N-Quads (canonicalization included), Turtle and TriG suites pass in full, beside the RDF 1.1 suites.
- **Semantics:**
  - Simple interpretations carry the injective triple denotation `IT`.
  - `rdf:dirLangString` is the third recognized datatype.
  - `rdfs:Proposition` holds every value of `IT`.
  - `rdf:reifies` is a property whose range is `rdfs:Proposition`.
- **Rules:**
  - 80 RDFS rules, each proven sound, including generalized rdfs14 (six `Grdfs14` rules) and rdfs14a.
  - Rule patterns can match inside triple terms.
- **Regimes:**
  - `simpleRules` with `checkQuery_sound_simple`, for well-formed queries.
  - `rdfRules` with `checkQuery_sound_rdf`, proven against the RDF conditions alone.
- **W3C RDF 1.2 Semantics suite:** 20 tests run in `leanrdf-elpi`. 16 positive tests are certified under their regime, and 4 negative tests are not derived; the non-assertion of a triple term is also proven by a countermodel. 9 tests need `xsd:integer` or `rdf:JSON` recognized and are listed as not run.
- **Downstream:** SPARQL, SHACL, OWL, the Elpi bridge, certificates and the oxilite bridge all accept triple terms as ordinary terms. SPARQL result formats read and write them.
- **Example:** `examples/rdf12-reification/` shows an asserted annotation, a reified triple that is not asserted, and propositions.

## Breaking changes (Lean API)

- `Term` gains the constructor `triple`, and `Literal` gains `dirLang`; exhaustive matches need new arms.
- `SimpleInterp` gains `IT`, `IT_wt` and `IT_inj`. `DInterp` gains `dirLangVal`, `dirLangVal_inj` and `IL_dirLang`. `RDFConditions`, `RDFSConditions` and `RDFSCore` gain the RDF 1.2 conditions.
- `Pat` gains `triple`, and `Guard` gains `dirLangLiteral`.
- The canonical N-Triples serializer writes TAB as `\t` and other control characters as `\uXXXX`.
- `denote` is `noncomputable`: a triple term has a referent only on `IT`'s domain.

## What is proven and what is trusted

- **Proven:** every theorem is checked by Lean's kernel. The library has no `sorry` and uses only `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`.
- **Trusted, not proven:**
  - The transcription of the W3C definitions, which are Candidate Recommendation and Working Drafts of 2026. Departures are listed in `tg.md/simplifications.md`.
  - The parsers, which are tested against the W3C suites.
  - The Lean compiler, wherever a certificate is checked by compiled code.
- **Not claimed:** completeness. "Not derived" never means "not entailed".
